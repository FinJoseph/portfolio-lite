## Overview

**ROI University of Management** is a 100% responsive static brochure website built without any frameworks or external libraries. It serves as a digital storefront for a management university based in Antananarivo, Madagascar.

The project was designed as a **prototype** to present the design and user experience to the client before developing the final version.

## Pages

- **Home**: Hero with Ken Burns animation, animated counters, featured programs, testimonials
- **About**: History, mission, vision, values, team
- **Programs**: 7 programs with filters (Bachelor/Master/MBA)
- **Student Life**: Photo gallery, events
- **Admissions**: 3-step process, fees, pre-registration
- **Contact**: Form, Google Maps, info

## Features

- **Zero dependencies**: no frameworks, no libraries
- **Centralized content**: all text editable in `js/data.js`
- **Scroll animations**: IntersectionObserver, animated counters
- **Responsive design**: custom breakpoints (992px, 768px, 480px)
- **32 images**: all ready to be replaced by the client
- **Maintainable**: client can edit content without touching HTML
