## Overview

**Art Malagasy** is an e-commerce brochure website for selling **traditional Malagasy artisan crafts**. It showcases handmade products by local artisans based in Fianarantsoa, Madagascar.

The project was built as a **prototype** to present the design, product catalog and user experience to the client before the final version.

## Pages

- **Home**: Hero slider, presentation, newsletter
- **About**: Product description
- **Products**: 6 products (woven art, baskets, hats, raffia, horn spoons)
- **Artisans**: Carousel featuring artisans (Boto, Soa)
- **Contact**: Form, Google Maps, social links

## Featured Products

- Sary sikotra (traditional woven art)
- Fiber baskets
- Traditional hats
- Raffia hats
- Raffia baskets
- Zebu horn spoons
